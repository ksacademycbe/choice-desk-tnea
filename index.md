# ChoiceDesk TNEA — Support

ChoiceDesk TNEA helps TNEA students and parents plan Tamil Nadu engineering counselling with the lowest and highest marks allotted in TNEA 2026 for every college, course, community and quota. It works offline, with no account and no ads.

## Contact

For help, feedback or to report a data error, email **ksacademy.cbe@gmail.com**.
Please include your iPhone or iPad model and the app version (Settings ▸ Data, privacy & help).

## Frequently asked questions

**Is this the official TNEA app?**
No. ChoiceDesk TNEA is an independent reference app. Always fill your final choices on the official TNEA portal, tneaonline.org.

**Are the marks a prediction?**
No. They are the lowest and highest marks allotted in TNEA 2026, taken from the official provisional allotment lists. Next year’s cutoffs will differ.

**Why is my community’s mark different from what I expected?**
The official lists count each seat under its allotted community. OC seats are open to everyone, so a BC, MBC or SC candidate with a high mark is often allotted an OC seat and is counted under OC. If you are not OC, check the OC figures as well.

**How is my cutoff worked out?**
Cutoff = Maths + Physics ÷ 2 + Chemistry ÷ 2, out of 200. You can type your cutoff or enter the three marks.

**How do I restore ChoiceDesk Pro on a new device?**
Open Settings (gear icon) ▸ ChoiceDesk Pro ▸ **Restore purchases**, signed in with the same Apple ID.

**My saved choices are missing after reinstalling.**
Turn on iCloud for ChoiceDesk TNEA in the iOS Settings app and open the app again. Data may take a few seconds to return.

**How do I change or remove my location?**
Settings (gear icon) ▸ Your location.

**A college is in the wrong place on the map.**
Some pins mark only the PIN-code area and say “Approximate”. Email us the college name and its correct location and we will fix it in the next update.
