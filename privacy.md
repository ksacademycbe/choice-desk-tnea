# Privacy Policy — ChoiceDesk TNEA

**Effective date:** 17 November 2026
**Developer:** Saravanakumar K

ChoiceDesk TNEA (“the app”) is an offline reference app for TNEA (Tamil Nadu Engineering Admissions) counselling. This policy explains what information the app uses and where it is kept.

## Summary

- We do not collect, store or share any personal data on our servers. The app has no server, no account and no sign-in.
- The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and no third-party SDKs.
- Everything you enter stays on your device and in your own private iCloud storage, which only your Apple ID can access.

## Information you enter in the app

| Information | Why it is used | Where it is kept |
|---|---|---|
| TNEA cutoff mark or subject marks, community, quota and search filters | To compare courses with published allotment marks | On your device and in your iCloud |
| Saved choices and their order | To build your choice list | On your device and in your iCloud |
| Name and profile photo (optional) | To personalise the home screen greeting | On your device and in your iCloud |
| Theme preference | To show light or dark mode | On your device and in your iCloud |
| Your town or approximate location (optional) | To show the distance to each college | On your device and in your iCloud |

We cannot see or access any of this information.

## iCloud backup

If iCloud is turned on for the app on your device, the information listed above is synced to Apple’s iCloud key-value storage under your Apple ID, so it can be restored when you reinstall the app or use another iPhone or iPad. This storage is private to your Apple ID and is governed by Apple’s privacy policy. You can turn it off in the iOS Settings app under your Apple ID ▸ iCloud.

## Location

Location is optional. The app asks for permission only when you tap **Use current location** in Settings. It reads one approximate location (about 1 km) and keeps only the town name and a rounded coordinate. You can type your town instead, or remove it at any time in Settings ▸ Your location. You can turn off location access in the iOS Settings app.

When you look up a typed town, view a road distance or tap **Directions**, the town name, the college name or the start and end coordinates are sent to Apple’s mapping services (Apple Maps) to get the result. These requests are handled by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy and are not sent to us.

## Camera and photos

If you choose a profile photo, the app uses the iOS photo picker or camera only for that photo. The photo is reduced to a small image and kept on your device and in your iCloud. It is never uploaded to us.

## In-app purchase

ChoiceDesk Pro is a one-time purchase processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether the purchase is active on your Apple ID.

## Printing, PDF and sharing

Reports you print, save as PDF or share are created on your device. They go only where you choose to send them using the iOS share sheet or printer.

## Links to other websites

The app contains links to official websites, such as the TNEA portal and its published documents, and to college websites. These open in Safari and are governed by the privacy policies of those websites.

## Children

The app is suitable for students. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Data deletion

Because all data stays on your device and in your iCloud, you are in full control:

- Remove a saved choice, your location or your photo in the app at any time.
- Deleting the app removes its data from your device.
- To remove the iCloud copy, go to the iOS Settings app ▸ your name ▸ iCloud ▸ Manage Account Storage, or turn off iCloud for the app before deleting it.

## Data source and disclaimer

Admission data in the app is taken from the public TNEA 2026 documents published on tneaonline.org: the provisional allotment lists, the college directory and the seat matrix. College map locations come from OpenStreetMap contributors, Wikidata, the Tamil Nadu Geographic Information System (TNGIS), college websites and GeoNames. ChoiceDesk TNEA is an independent app and is not affiliated with TNEA, the Directorate of Technical Education (DoTE), Anna University, the Government of Tamil Nadu or any college.
